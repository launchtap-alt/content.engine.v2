How to Use the
Launch Tap Content Engine
A complete walkthrough of every field, every button, and every output — so you can go from blank page to three ready-to-post scripts in under two minutes.

Understanding the Input Fields

The Script Writer has four core input fields. Think of them as your brief. The more specific you are, the more human and powerful your scripts will be.

Field 01 — Your Topic or Offer Required
This is what your script is about. Your product, your service, your story, or your angle. Be specific — the more concrete your topic, the more concrete your hook.
I sell a ₱99 digital bundle with 99+ products and full reseller rights — anyone can buy and resell it to earn.
Field 02 — What Your Audience Needs to Understand Required
What belief shift does your audience need to make before they will buy? What do they currently misunderstand about your offer? This becomes the body of your script.
They think starting an online business needs thousands of pesos and months of learning. But they can start with ₱99 today and already have 99+ products ready to sell.
Field 03 — A Real Example or Proof Power-Up
A specific result, a testimonial, a real number, a real person's story. This is your social proof. Scripts with specific examples convert dramatically higher than vague claims.
A college student earned ₱3,200 in her first week just by sharing the link on her Facebook stories with zero paid ads.
Field 04 — Why They Should Act Now Power-Up
Urgency and scarcity live here. What happens if they wait? What opportunity do they miss? This powers your CTA and makes "I'll think about it" feel irrational.
Every day without a digital product is another day trading time for money. This bundle removes that excuse permanently — for less than a meal at Jollibee.
💡 Pro Tip
You do not need to be a writer to fill these fields. Just answer them the way you would explain your offer to a friend over text. The system transforms your raw answers into a polished, human-sounding script. That is the entire point.

Choosing Your Tone, Language & Niche

Before hitting Generate, configure these three settings to dial in your output.

A
Select Your Tone Chips
Choose one or more tones from the chip selector. Options include Relatable, Motivational, Hype, Storytelling, Educational, Direct, and more. Your tone is the emotional register of your script. A "Motivational + Storytelling" combination hits different than pure "Direct." Pick what fits your content and your audience's mood.
B
Choose Your Language
Select Taglish for the classic Filipino-English mix that your audience already speaks. Choose Filipino for full Tagalog, or English for a broader reach. Every output shows both the script language and an English translation side by side — so you always know what you're posting.
C
Set Your Niche
Choose the niche that best matches your audience: Digital Products, Online Business, Student Side Hustle, Freelancing, and more. This tells the AI who is watching your video — and scripts every hook, body line, and CTA accordingly.

Reading Your Output: Three Scripts, One Session

Every generation produces three complete scripts. Here is what each one is designed to do.

1
Script 1 — Hook (Revelation Format)
Opens with a paradox or counterintuitive truth that stops the scroll in the first two seconds. Designed for maximum reach and cold audience capture. Use this when you want views from people who don't know you yet.
2
Script 2 — Confession (Identity Format)
Opens with vulnerability and a personal admission. Creates instant trust with warm audiences who already follow you. Use this for your existing community or when you want to build deeper connection.
3
Script 3 — Story (Narrative Format)
A real-world Filipino scenario or analogy that drops your concept into a scene the audience can see and feel. Use this for shares and saves — stories are the most viral format on TikTok and Reels.
✓ What You Get Per Script
Each output includes: bilingual lines (your language + English), segment labels (Hook / Body / CTA), an on-screen text overlay suggestion, and a director's notes section with hashtags and posting tips. Everything you need from page to post — in one output.

Module Three
Fixing Errors —
When Something Goes Wrong
The Script Writer is reliable — but like any tool powered by an API, occasional errors happen. This module covers every common issue and gives you the exact prompt to fix each one, fast.
⚠ Error Type 1 — API Key Not Working / Invalid Key
What it looks like: The status indicator shows red, or you see an error message saying "Invalid API Key," "Unauthorized," or "401 Error" after clicking Generate.
!
Why This Happens
You either copied the key incorrectly (with an extra space at the start or end), the key was generated but not yet saved, or the key was revoked in your Anthropic console. Claude.ai artifacts also do not save API keys between sessions — so you must re-enter it each time you reload the artifact.
📋 Prompt — Fix API Key Error
The Script Writer artifact is showing an API key error (red status / "Invalid Key" message). Please check the artifact and confirm: 1. The API key input field is visible and functional 2. The Save button correctly stores the key in the artifact's state 3. The key is being passed correctly in the Authorization header as: Bearer [key] 4. There are no extra spaces trimming the key value If any of these are broken, fix them and re-render the artifact so I can re-enter my key. My API key starts with: sk-ant-api03-
⚠ Error Type 2 — Generate Button Does Nothing / Spinner Loops Forever
What it looks like: You click Generate, the spinner appears, but scripts never load. Or nothing happens at all when you click the button.
📋 Prompt — Fix Generation Loop / Dead Button
The Script Writer artifact is not generating scripts. The Generate button either does nothing or the loading spinner runs indefinitely without showing output. Please debug the artifact and check: 1. The fetchOneScript() function is correctly calling the Anthropic API at: https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages 2. The API request includes the required headers: - Content-Type: application/json - x-api-key: [stored key] - anthropic-version: 2023-06-01 - anthropic-dangerous-direct-browser-access: true 3. The JSON response is being correctly parsed from data.content[0].text 4. The loading state visibility is toggling correctly after generation completes 5. Error handling is showing the error box if the API returns a non-200 response Fix whichever of these is broken and re-render the working artifact.
⚠ Error Type 3 — Scripts Generated But Display Looks Broken
What it looks like: Scripts appear but the layout is wrong — text is overlapping, bilingual columns are broken, or the script tabs are not switching correctly.
📋 Prompt — Fix Display / Rendering Bug
The Script Writer is generating scripts but the output display is broken. Please check and fix the following in the artifact: 1. The output panel tab system (Script 1 / Script 2 / Script 3) is not switching correctly — ensure showScript() is toggling the .active class on both tabs and script blocks 2. The bilingual grid layout (.script-line-pair with two columns) is rendering correctly — check that the grid-template-columns:1fr 1fr CSS is intact 3. The .output-content element is receiving the .visible class after generation 4. The renderScript() function is correctly building and injecting HTML into the correct script-N-content container IDs Re-render the fixed artifact at full width so both panels are visible.
⚠ Error Type 4 — History / Sessions Not Saving
What it looks like: You generate scripts, but the Saved Sessions bar is always empty. Or sessions disappear when you close and reopen the artifact.
📋 Prompt — Fix Session History / localStorage
The Script Writer's session history is not saving or restoring correctly. Please check and fix the following: 1. The saveSession() function is being called after all three scripts finish generating 2. localStorage.setItem() is being called with the correct STORAGE_KEY 3. The getSessions() function is reading from the same key without errors 4. The renderHistoryBar() function is being called both on DOMContentLoaded and after saveSession() 5. The loadSession() function correctly restores fields, tones, and script content when a chip is clicked Note: If localStorage is blocked in this environment, implement in-memory session storage as a fallback and re-render the fixed artifact.
⚠ Error Type 5 — Full Reset (When Nothing Else Works)
What it looks like: Multiple errors, the artifact is unresponsive, or something changed that broke the whole tool. Use this as your nuclear option.
📋 Prompt — Full Artifact Reset
The Launch Tap Script Writer artifact is not working correctly and I need a full reset. Please re-render the original HTML file I uploaded at the start of this conversation, exactly as it was designed, with no modifications. Requirements for the re-render: - Full two-panel layout (input left, output right) - All original CSS variables and galaxy navy design intact - API key bar visible at the top with gold border - History bar below the header - All four intake fields functional with auto-save - Tone chips, language selector, and niche selector working - Generate button triggering the three-script generation sequence - Output tabs (Script 1 / 2 / 3) switching correctly - Copy button per script working Render at maximum width. Do not simplify. Do not strip functionality. This is a production tool, not a demo.

Before You Report an Error — Quick Self-Check

  • My API key is entered in the gold API Key Bar and the status dot is green
  • I am using a valid Anthropic API key starting with sk-ant-api03-
  • I have filled in at least Field 1 (Topic) and Field 2 (Understanding) before generating
  • I have selected at least one Tone chip
  • I have chosen a Language and a Niche from the dropdowns
  • I waited at least 60 seconds for generation to complete before concluding it is broken
  • If the artifact looks visually broken, I tried scrolling — sometimes the output panel is below the fold on mobile
💡 The Fastest Fix Every Time
If nothing else works: start a fresh Claude conversation, re-upload the HTML file, use the "Open as Live Artifact" prompt from Module 2, and re-enter your API key. The Script Writer is stateless — each new session is clean. Your saved sessions live in your browser's localStorage, not in Claude.
